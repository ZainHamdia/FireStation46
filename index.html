Frequently Asked Questions

\n

Find answers to common questions about Station 46 and volunteering.

\n
\n \n
\n \n
\n \n Do I need any prior firefighting or emergency experience to join?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

No prior experience is necessary! We welcome members from all backgrounds and skill levels. Station 46 provides 100% of your personal protective turnout gear, in-house mentorship, and formal academy training (such as NJ State Firefighter 1 certification) at absolutely no cost to you.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n What is the time commitment expected of a volunteer firefighter?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

We understand our members balance full-time jobs, families, and academic schedules. Regular company drills and business meetings take place on Wednesday evenings at 7:00 PM. Emergency calls occur 24/7/365, and members respond when available using modern smartphone alert systems and pagers.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n What are the age requirements to join Station 46?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

Standard firefighting and associate membership is open to anyone 18 years of age and older. For high school students ages 16 to 18, we offer an active Junior Firefighter Program that teaches hands-on skills, emergency operations, and leadership while prioritizing school and family.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n Can I join if I don't want to fight interior fires?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

Yes, absolutely! There are numerous critical roles that do not involve interior structure firefighting. You can serve as an Exterior Firefighter, Apparatus Driver / Pump Operator, Fire Police / Traffic Control Officer, or an Associate Member assisting with administration, IT, community outreach, and station logistics.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n How does Station 46 get dispatched to emergencies?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

When 911 is dialed in Montgomery Township or surrounding areas, Somerset County Emergency Communications alerts our station. Volunteer members receive real-time notifications on pagers and dispatch apps, quickly report to the firehouse at 529 Route 518 in Skillman, don their turnout gear, and respond on the appropriate apparatus.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n How is Station 46 funded, and are donations tax-deductible?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2 is an independent, 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. While we receive municipal assistance for baseline operational expenses, we rely heavily on community contributions and annual fund drives to purchase lifesaving rescue gear and maintain our fleet. All donations are 100% tax-deductible and can be made through our Donate section.

\n
\n
\n\n \n
\n \n How can I apply or visit the fire station?\n \n \n \n \n
\n

You can apply online anytime by completing the application form on our Membership page. You are also warmly invited to stop by the firehouse any Wednesday at 7:00 PM (529 Route 518, Skillman, NJ 08558) to meet the crew, tour the apparatus bays, and ask questions in person!
