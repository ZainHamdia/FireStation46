Why Volunteer at Station 46?

You don't need prior emergency experience to save lives. Our members are local high school students, engineers, teachers, parents, and retirees who stepped up to protect Montgomery.

Zero Cost, 100% Free

We provide all your custom-fitted turnout gear, boots, helmet, and formal NJ State Firefighter 1 academy certification at absolutely no cost.

Fits Around Your Life

No mandatory shift minimums. Smartphone dispatch alerts let you respond to emergency calls when you're available around work, school, and family.

A Second Family

Join an exceptional crew rooted in mutual respect, teamwork, and community pride. Gain lifelong friendships, mentorship, and camaraderie.

Roles for Everyone

Not interested in fighting interior structure fires? Serve as an apparatus operator, scene support crew, fire police officer, or administrative associate.

Find Your Path to Serve

From frontline suppression and apparatus operation to high school leadership and station support, find the role that matches your passion.

Ages 18+ • Frontline

Active Firefighter

Operate fire apparatus, perform interior/exterior fire suppression, vehicle extrication, and emergency search and rescue operations.

  • NJ State Firefighter 1 certification fully paid by Station 46
  • Train on engines, aerial ladder truck, rescue pumpers, and marine craft
  • Flexible response with in-house drill nights on Wednesdays
Apply as Firefighter
Ages 16–18 • High School

Junior Firefighter

Designed specifically for students aged 16–18. Build leadership, emergency skills, and college-ready character while keeping school first.

  • Hands-on training with fire tools, hoses, ground ladders, and gear
  • Strict school-first priority (no school-night curfew conflicts)
  • Fast-track pathway to full adult firefighter status at age 18

All Ages 18+ • Non-Interior

Support & Associate

Support emergency scenes and department logistics without interior firefighting. Critical community roles that keep Station 46 moving.

  • Fire Police, scene perimeter safety, and traffic control
  • Apparatus driving, pump operation, and rehabilitation supply
  • Administration, IT, community events, and Operation Santa

Support Station 46

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2 is an all-volunteer, 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Your generous donations help us continue to protect lives and property.

Your Support Saves Lives

Unlike career fire departments, we rely on the generosity of our community members, businesses, and municipal support to fund our daily operations and equipment. Your contributions directly fund:

  • Protective Gear: Outfitting a single volunteer firefighter with bunker gear, helmet, boots, and SCBA respirator costs over $10,000.
  • Rescue Tools: Thermal cameras, gas meters, hydraulic vehicle extrication tools, and swift water rescue equipment.
  • Continuous Training: Regular drills, fire academy certification courses, and specialized rescue training.
