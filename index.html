Ways to Volunteer

Station 46 is staffed entirely by volunteers from our township. Whether you want to fight fires, manage scene safety, or assist behind the scenes, there is a vital role for everyone.

Ages 18+ • Active Firefighter

Firefighter

Respond to structural fires, motor vehicle accidents, alarms, and emergency rescues across Montgomery Township.

  • NJ State Firefighter 1 certification fully paid by Station 46
  • Train on engines, ladder trucks, rescue pumpers, and marine craft
  • Weekly company training drills every Wednesday at 7:00 PM
Apply as Firefighter
Ages 16–18 • Trainee

Junior Firefighter

For trainees ages 16–18. Learn firefighting fundamentals, earn community service hours, and prepare to become full members.

  • Hands-on training with fire tools, hoses, ground ladders, and gear
  • Strict academic-first priority (no late night curfew conflicts)
  • Fast-track pathway to full adult firefighter status at age 18
Apply as Trainee
Ages 18+ • Non-Firefighting

Fire Police

Protect operating firefighters, EMS, and the public by managing roadway closures, detours, and incident scene safety.

  • Roadway safety closures, scene perimeters, and traffic direction
  • NJ Fire Police Academy certification, gear, and radios provided
  • Crucial scene protection role without interior structure firefighting
Apply for Fire Police
Ages 18+ • Support, Non-Interior

Associate Member

Support Station 46 behind the scenes through logistics, administration, facility maintenance, and community event coordination.

  • Station maintenance, logistics, and incident hydration/rehab
  • Coordinate Operation Santa deliveries, open houses, and fund drives
  • Executive board, bookkeeping, technology, and communications

Support Station 46

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2 is an all-volunteer, 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Your generous support helps us continue to protect lives and property.

Your Support Saves Lives

Unlike career fire departments, we rely on the generosity of our community members, businesses, and municipal support to fund our daily operations and equipment. Your contributions directly fund:

  • Protective Gear: Outfitting a single volunteer firefighter with bunker gear, helmet, boots, and SCBA respirator costs over $10,000.
  • Rescue Tools: Thermal cameras, gas meters, hydraulic vehicle extrication tools, and swift water rescue equipment.
  • Continuous Training: Regular drills, fire academy certification courses, and specialized rescue training.

Other Ways to Support

There are meaningful ways to help Station 46 beyond financial contributions. We welcome vehicle donations, structural drill properties, and clothing/book donations.

Totaled / Junk Cars for Drills

Have an old, junk, or totaled vehicle? Donate it to Station 46 so our firefighters can practice life-saving vehicle extrication drills and hydraulic rescue tool techniques.

Inquire About Donating a Car →

Houses Slated for Demolition

If you have a residential or commercial building scheduled to be demolished, allow our crew to conduct search-and-rescue, ventilation, and hose advancement drills before tear-down.

We host community book and clothing collection bins right on-site at the firehouse (529 Route 518). Drop off your gently used books, clothes, and shoes anytime 24/7 to help those less fortunate.
