Bringing Holiday Magic to Our Community

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2 is arranging to have Santa Claus and his elves deliver Christmas gifts by fire engine. The volunteers are anxiously planning to bring this special event to our community.

Due to high demand, we are delivering gifts exclusively into the Blawenburg / Skillman / Fire District #2 coverage area.

Quick Facts & Summary

  • Drop-Off Dates: Dec 16, 17, & 18 (5 PM – 9 PM)
  • Delivery Date: Sunday, December 21, 2025 (10 AM – 6 PM)
  • Donation: $25 minimum per household
  • Delivery Area: Skillman, Blawenburg & District #2

Participation Guidelines

Please review these simple steps to ensure Santa's delivery run is smooth and safe for all children.

1

Household Donation

A minimum donation of $25 per household is requested to support the volunteer fire company. Payment is due upon gift drop off (Cash, Check, or Credit Card accepted).

2

Wrapped & Labeled Gift

Please wrap one small gift per child and label it clearly with the child's full name and exact delivery street address.

3

Drop-Off at Station 46

Bring your gift(s) to The Firehouse, located at 529 Route 518 in Skillman during the drop-off hours (Dec 16–18 between 5 PM and 9 PM).

4

Completed Contact Form

Submit your registration using the online form below, or download and complete the paper contact form, and bring it with you when you drop off your gift(s) at the firehouse.
